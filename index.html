[ about ]

I'm a self-taught security student and researcher. I learned by breaking things in labs, reading other people's writeups, and taking apart samples until I could explain what they did and why.

I work on both sides: finding and exploiting weaknesses, then working out how to detect and stop them. I'm documenting the process here, on the way to a cybersecurity degree.
